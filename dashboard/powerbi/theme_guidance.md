# CreditGuard Power BI Theme Guidance

Use the following color palette to ensure a professional, clean, and financially-appropriate aesthetic.

## Primary Colors
- **Header Backgrounds & Key Borders**: Dark Navy `#1B263B`
- **Primary Data Visuals (Bars/Lines)**: Medium Blue `#415A77`
- **Secondary Data Visuals**: Muted Blue/Grey `#778DA9`

## Status & Risk Colors
- **Default / High Risk (Bad)**: Deep Red `#E63946`
  - Use strictly for indicating defaulters or highly dangerous financial metrics.
- **Warning / Medium Risk**: Vibrant Orange `#F4A261`
  - Use for delays or moderate credit utilization alerts.
- **Healthy / Low Risk (Good)**: Forest Green `#2A9D8F`
  - Use for non-defaulters, fully paid statements, and healthy utilization.

## Background & Typography
- **Dashboard Canvas Background**: Light Neutral `#F8F9FA` or Very Light Grey `#E0E1DD`.
- **Card/Visual Backgrounds**: Pure White `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Text**: Dark Charcoal `#1A1A1D`.
- **Secondary Text (Labels/Axes)**: Medium Grey `#6C757D`.

## Application
1. In Power BI, go to the **View** ribbon.
2. Click the dropdown under Themes and select **Customize current theme**.
3. Input the hex codes above into the color palette to apply them globally across all charts.
